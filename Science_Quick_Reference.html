Weather

TopicsCoriolis · Pressure · Heat dome
Coriolis effect
Northern Hemisphere storms spin counterclockwise; Southern Hemisphere storms spin clockwise
Low pressure
Rising air, spiraling inward (counterclockwise in the NH); clouds and precipitation
High pressure
Sinking air, spiraling outward (clockwise in the NH); clear, stable weather
Heat dome
A persistent high-pressure ridge through the troposphere (~10 km), often under a stalled bulge in the jet stream
How a heat dome works
  • Sinking air compresses and warms.
  • No clouds, so more solar heating.
  • The ridge acts as a lid, trapping the heat.
