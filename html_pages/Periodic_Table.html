Atom & Molecule

TopicsSize · Nucleus · Protons · Neutrons · Electrons
Atom
The smallest unit of an element that keeps its properties; ~0.1 – 0.5 nm across (1 nm = 10⁻⁹ m) and mostly empty space
Nucleus
The dense center; holds nearly all of the atom's mass
Protons
Positive charge; their count (the atomic number) defines the element
Neutrons
No charge; add mass and stabilize the nucleus; different neutron counts give different isotopes
Electrons
Negative charge, ~1/1,836 the mass of a proton; occupy orbitals around the nucleus and determine chemical behavior and bonding
Molecule
Two or more atoms joined by chemical bonds (sharing or transferring electrons); the smallest unit of a compound that keeps its properties, e.g. H₂O (2 hydrogen + 1 oxygen) or O₂ (2 oxygen)

Atomic Number vs. Atomic Mass

TopicsZ · Mass number · Isotopes
Atomic number (Z)
The number of protons in the nucleus; identifies the element, is always a whole number, and never changes for a given element. Example: carbon = 6
Atomic mass
The total mass of the atom, mostly protons plus neutrons; measured in atomic mass units (u); usually a decimal because it averages the element's isotopes. Example: carbon = 12.011 u
Mass number (A)
The whole-number count of protons plus neutrons in one specific isotope. Example: carbon-12, A = 12 (6 protons + 6 neutrons)
