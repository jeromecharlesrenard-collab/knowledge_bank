Trading Sessions

TopicsPre-market · Regular hours · After hours · Extended hours
pm
Pre-market — the session before the open, roughly 4:00 – 9:30 a.m. ET (exact start varies by broker)
rth
Regular trading hours — 9:30 a.m. – 4:00 p.m. ET for US stocks and options
ah
After hours — the session after the close, roughly 4:00 – 8:00 p.m. ET (exact end varies by broker)
eth
Extended trading hours — pre-market and after hours combined; any trading outside RTH

Order Types & Entry

TopicsMarket · Limit · Stop · Trigger · Order entry tools
mkt
Market order — fills immediately at the best available price
lmt
Limit order — fills only at a specified price or better
stp
Stop order — sits inactive until its trigger price is reached, then becomes a market order (a stop-limit order if a limit price is attached)
tgr
Trigger — the price level that activates a conditional order, such as a stop
oet
Order entry tools — the on-screen controls used to place and manage orders (e.g., on thinkorswim)

Market Data & Analysis

TopicsCurrent price · Market information · Timeframe · Volume footprint · Option symbol
cp
Current price — the last traded price of a security
mi
Market information — quotes, volume, and other data describing a security
tf
Timeframe — the interval of each bar or candle on a chart (1-minute, daily, etc.)
vfp
Volume footprint — a chart showing traded volume at each price level inside a bar, used to read buying and selling pressure
ops
Option symbol — the contract identifier for an option: underlying, expiration, strike, and call/put

Platforms & Tools

Topicsthinkorswim · TradingView · TraderSync · Active trader
tos
thinkorswim — charting and order-entry platform from Charles Schwab (formerly TD Ameritrade)
tdv
TradingView — charting and social-trading platform
tds
TraderSync — trade-journal and performance-analytics platform
at
Active trader — a trader who places frequent trades; also used as the name of some brokers' active-trading platform or dashboard
