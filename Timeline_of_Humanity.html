~7 – 6M years ago

Human Evolution (Hominin Species)

Span~3.9M years ago – present
~3.9 – 2.9M years ago
Australopithecus afarensis; the "Lucy" fossil is dated 3.2M years (Ethiopia)
~2.4 – 1.4M years ago
Homo habilis ("Handy Man"), associated with Oldowan stone tools
~1.9M – 110K years ago
Homo erectus ("Upright Man"), the first to expand out of Africa; likely early fire use
~700 – 200K years ago
Homo heidelbergensis, likely the common ancestor of Sapiens and Neanderthals (debated)
~400 – 40K years ago
Neanderthals: lineage from ~400K, classic form ~130K, extinct ~40K
~400 – 30K years ago
Denisovans (Asia), known mainly from DNA
~300K years ago – present
Homo sapiens (modern humans)

Origin of Homo sapiens

Span~315K – 233K years ago
Consensus
~300K years ago; the earlier view was ~200K years ago in East Africa
Jebel Irhoud, Morocco
~300 – 315K years ago; the oldest known Sapiens, with a modern face and an elongated braincase
Florisbad, South Africa
~260K years ago; partial cranium, an early transitional form
Omo Kibish, Ethiopia
~233K years ago; among the earliest complete East African examples
Pan-African model
Interconnected populations across Africa, mixing genetically and culturally over hundreds of thousands of years, not a single East African cradle

Anatomical vs. Behavioral Modernity

Span~300K – 50K years ago
~300K years ago
Anatomically modern: flat face, chin, modern skeleton
~100 – 50K years ago
Behaviorally modern: symbolic thought, complex tools, art, language
~60 – 50K years ago
Main dispersal out of Africa; interbreeding with Neanderthals and Denisovans

Span~3.3M years ago – 3200 BC
~3.3M years ago
Oldest known stone tools (Lomekwi, Kenya)
~1M years ago
Earliest evidence of fire use (Wonderwerk Cave, South Africa)
~400K years ago
Habitual controlled fire
~50 – 45K years ago
Oldest figurative cave art (Sulawesi, Indonesia)
~9500 BC
Agriculture begins (Fertile Crescent)
~3200 BC
Writing invented (Sumerian cuneiform)

Early Civilizations

Span~3500 BC – AD 1521
~3500 BC
Sumer, Mesopotamia: the first cities (Uruk)
~3100 BC
Egypt unified (Narmer); New Kingdom empire from ~1550 BC
~2600 BC
Indus Valley civilization at its peak
~1600 BC
Shang dynasty, China
~1500 – 400 BC
Olmec, Gulf Coast of Mexico: the first civilization of Mesoamerica (central Mexico to western Honduras, Nicaragua, and Costa Rica)
~800 BC
Archaic Greece: city-states; an empire only under Alexander, 336 – 323 BC

Rome: founded 753 BC, Republic 509 BC, Empire 27 BC (Augustus), fall of the West AD 476
