Charging the Model 3 and Model Y: 10% to 80%

Charging from 10% to 80% adds 70% of the battery's usable capacity. For the Long Range versions, that is about 57 kWh for the Model 3 (~82 kWh pack) and 53 kWh for the Model Y (~75 kWh pack). Home charging draws roughly 10% more from the grid because of charging losses. Below is the breakdown for each model, at home and at a Tesla Supercharger.

3

Tesla Model 3

Long Range, ~57 kWh added (~63 kWh from the grid at home)

Home Time5.5 – 8 hours
Home Cost$10 – $16
Supercharger Time25 – 35 minutes
Supercharger Cost$20 – $29
At Home
  • Tesla Wall Connector (11.5 kW / 48A): ~5 hours, 30 minutes
  • 240V / 32A outlet (7.7 kW): ~8 hours, 15 minutes
  • Standard 120V outlet: adds only ~3 – 4 miles of range per hour, so it would take about 45 hours
  • US average rate (~$0.16 – $0.25/kWh): ~$10.00 – $15.75
  • French EDF home rate (~€0.16 – €0.20/kWh): ~€10.00 – €12.60
At a Supercharger (V3 / V4)
  • Charging peaks at up to 250 kW near 10%, then slows gradually as the battery fills to 80%.
  • US rate (~$0.35 – $0.50/kWh): ~$20.00 – $28.50
  • European rate (~€0.33 – €0.41/kWh): ~€18.80 – €23.40

Tesla Model Y

Home Time5 – 7.5 hours
Home Cost$9 – $15
Supercharger Time30 – 35 minutes
Supercharger Cost$19 – $27
At Home
  • Tesla Wall Connector (11.5 kW / 48A): ~5 hours
  • 240V / 32A outlet (7.7 kW): ~7 hours, 30 minutes
  • Standard 120V outlet: adds only ~3 miles of range per hour, so it would take about 41 hours
  • US average rate (~$0.16 – $0.25/kWh): ~$9.30 – $14.50
  • French EDF home rate (~€0.16 – €0.20/kWh): ~€9.30 – €11.60
At a Supercharger (V3 / V4)
  • Charging peaks at up to 250 kW near 10%. The larger, heavier SUV averages a little slower than the Model 3 over the session.
  • US rate (~$0.35 – $0.50/kWh): ~$18.50 – $26.50
  • European rate (~€0.33 – €0.41/kWh): ~€17.50 – €21.70

Off-peak overnight home charging can cut costs by another 20% – 40%. Supercharger prices vary with location, time of day, and peak vs. off-peak hours.
